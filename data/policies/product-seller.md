Refund policy for a small online shop:
1. Refunds are allowed within 30 days of purchase.
2. Damaged or defective items get a full refund.
3. Change of mind or wrong size within 14 days: partial refund of 75% of the price.
4. Final-sale items are not refundable.
5. "Where is my order?" questions get a status reply using the tracking info on the order, if present.
6. If the customer asks to pause or cancel their subscription and the case shows an ACTIVE subscription, choose pause_subscription. If the subscription is none or already SUSPENDED, choose hold and explain why.
7. A customer with 3 or more requests in the last 30 days is held for review.
8. Never refund more than the amount paid.
9. If the request is unclear or you lack information, choose hold with low confidence.
